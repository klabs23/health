Movement with a purpose

A gentle adult starting point
↗

MONDAY + THURSDAY

Full-body foundations

Start with 1 set of 8–12 comfortable repetitions. Add a second set as you feel ready.

  • Chair squats · legs & balance
  • Wall push-ups · upper body
  • Light resistance rows · back
  • Supported calf raises · lower legs

Use controlled movements, breathe normally, and stop for pain or dizziness. Allow at least a day between strength sessions.

〰

A LITTLE, MOST DAYS

Find your everyday rhythm

  • Start with a 10-minute comfortable walk
  • Build toward 150 moderate minutes weekly
  • Break up long periods of sitting
  • Try 5 minutes of gentle mobility

You should be able to talk during moderate exercise. Adapt activity with your clinician if you have a condition, injury, pregnancy, or new symptoms.

BMI is one screening measure, not a complete assessment of health or fitness. Focus on sustainable eating, sleep, movement, and strength rather than a single number.
